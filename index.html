Questions clients ask

If yours isn't here, ask it directly in the form below.

How long does a project take?

Most landing pages and small business sites take 5–10 days from approved design to launch. Online stores and web apps usually take 3–6 weeks depending on scope.

What's included in the price?

Design, development, mobile responsiveness, and a launch walkthrough. Domain, hosting, and any paid plugins or apps are quoted separately based on what your project needs.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes — every project includes rounds of revisions during the design stage, so you can adjust layout, colors, and content before anything goes live.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

I can set both up for you, or work with hosting and domains you already own. Either way, you'll always have full access and ownership.
